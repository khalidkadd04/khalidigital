## Project brief

Design a Google Ads Search campaign from scratch for *StudioPulse*, a fictional boutique fitness studio targeting urban professionals aged 25–40 in Casablanca.

## Keyword strategy

I used Google Keyword Planner and Semrush to build a three-tier keyword list:

- **Branded** — studio name + variants (low volume, high intent, cheap CPC).
- **Generic** — "fitness studio near me", "cours de yoga Casablanca" (high volume, competitive).
- **Long-tail** — "cours de pilates débutant Casablanca" (low competition, high conversion intent).

Match types: broad match for discovery in month 1, then pruned to phrase + exact based on search term report.

## Ad group structure

Each ad group maps to one service: Yoga, Pilates, HIIT, Personal Training. This keeps keyword–ad–landing page relevance tight, which directly lifts Quality Score.

## Sample RSA headlines

> **Headline 1:** Cours de Yoga à Casablanca  
> **Headline 2:** Réservez votre 1re séance gratuite  
> **Headline 3:** Studio boutique — 15 places max
