## Brand brief

Maison Argan is a fictional premium Moroccan cosmetics brand — argan oil serums, body butters, and hair treatments — targeting French-speaking women aged 28–45 who value authenticity and sustainable sourcing.

## Market analysis

Competitors split into two camps: mass-market (Garnier, L'Oréal) with high ad spend but generic messaging, and niche indie brands with authentic storytelling but low reach. The gap: an authentic Moroccan heritage brand with consistent, elevated visual identity.

## Personas

| Persona | Age | Platform | Content Hook |
|---------|-----|----------|-------------|
| Sophie — urban professional | 32 | Instagram | Efficacy, minimal routine |
| Amina — diaspora connector | 28 | TikTok | Heritage, nostalgia |
| Claire — eco-conscious mom | 41 | Instagram | Ingredients, sustainability |

## Platform rationale

- **Instagram** — primary: product showcase, UGC reposting, Stories for Q&A.
- **TikTok** — secondary: behind-the-scenes in Morocco, ingredient origin videos, founder storytelling.

## Content pillars

1. **Origins** — sourcing stories, cooperative footage, Berber heritage.
2. **Ritual** — skincare routine demos, before/after, texture focus.
3. **Community** — customer reposts, reviews, founder replies.
4. **Education** — ingredient deep-dives, myth-busting, sustainability facts.
